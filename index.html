How to use this platform
A guide for new users
What problem does this solve?

Global supply chains involve hundreds of suppliers across dozens of countries. Managing communication with all of them — collecting compliance data, running risk assessments, tracking who responded and who didn't — is typically done through scattered emails and spreadsheets with no visibility.

This platform centralizes supplier communication management: you can track which suppliers responded to which campaigns, identify who is non-responsive, escalate gaps, and generate leadership-ready insights — all in one place.

Step 1 — Add your suppliers

Go to Suppliers and click Add supplier to manually add one at a time. Or go to Import data to bulk upload a CSV file with all your suppliers at once. Each supplier needs: name, region, tier, commodity, contact name, contact email, and current status (Responded / Pending / Escalated).

Step 2 — Create a campaign

Go to Campaigns and click New campaign. Name your campaign, set the type (compliance data, risk assessment, corrective action, etc.), set a due date, and choose which suppliers to target. The campaign will track how many suppliers responded vs. are still pending.

Step 3 — Update supplier status

As suppliers respond to your outreach, go to Suppliers and update their status. Use Responded when they've submitted the required data, Pending for those still waiting, and Escalated for suppliers who have been silent too long and need direct intervention.

Step 4 — Monitor and act

The Overview dashboard shows your response rate, active campaigns, and escalations in real time. The Intelligence tab generates gap analysis and a leadership-ready summary from your data. The Escalations tab shows all suppliers flagged for immediate action.

What this demonstrates

This tool was built to demonstrate the ability to design and manage scalable supplier communication programs — owning outreach strategy, driving supplier accountability, ensuring data integrity, and generating leadership-ready insights across a global supply base. These are the core capabilities required for supplier communications and intelligence program management roles in complex manufacturing environments.
