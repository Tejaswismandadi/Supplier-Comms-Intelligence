Emerging patterns and recommended actions
Asia Pacific consistently 10-15% below global average across all campaign types
Language and time zone barriers. Recommend localized outreach templates and regional follow-up agent for Asia Pacific suppliers.
Corrective action campaigns have highest response rate (93%) — highest supplier accountability
Urgency framing works. Apply same tone to compliance campaigns for Watch List and P1 suppliers.
Q1 response rates consistently highest across all regions and campaign types
Suppliers more responsive at fiscal year start. Schedule critical compliance campaigns in Q1 for best results.
Watch List suppliers show 0% response without category manager involvement from Stage 1
Program Manager outreach alone insufficient for Watch List. Co-own from day one with category manager.
Contact data decay approximately 15% per year — India and Asia Pacific highest churn
Semi-annual verification not sufficient for high-churn regions. Recommend quarterly verification for India and Asia Pacific.

REI method — Simchi-Levi Risk Exposure Index
REI = TTR minus TTS · Positive REI = supplier takes longer to recover than you can survive without them = critical risk
Critical P1
Single source or high REI. Escalate after 7 days.
Strategic P2
Spend above $2M. Escalate after 14 days.
Preferred P3
Spend above $500K. Escalate after 21 days.
Approved P4
Lower spend. Escalate after 30 days.
Watch List
Financial risk or failures. Escalate immediately.
The four campaign types
Regulatory Compliance
Quarterly and annual. All active suppliers. CO2, CBAM, conflict minerals, RoHS/REACH. Output: Compliance dashboard submitted to regulators.
Risk and Financial Health
Annual. P1 and P2 suppliers. REI inputs, financial health, business continuity. Output: Risk register updated, REI scores refreshed.
Performance and Corrective Action
Event-triggered. Root cause analysis, corrective action plans. Output: CAP accepted or supplier escalated to Watch List.
Contact and Master Data
Semi-annual. Full supply base. Contact verification, portal registration, code of conduct. Output: Database verified, bounces resolved in 48 hours.
